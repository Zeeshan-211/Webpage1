Project Showcase

Three projects across data analysis and web design, with the challenges, approaches, and lessons behind each one.

01

Website concept

Gauchini Restaurant

Role: project brief, content direction, and review.

Problem

A restaurant needs one clear place for guests to discover its atmosphere, explore the menu, learn its story, and plan a visit.

Approach

Defined a dark, elegant restaurant concept and organized it into connected Home, Menu, About, and Bookings pages.

Outcome

Delivered a responsive four-page prototype with social links, contact details, and a booking request preview clearly marked as non-live.

Skills and lesson

Content structure, responsive HTML and CSS, accessibility, and usability review. A clear path from discovery to booking helps guests decide what to do next.

02

Personal website

Portfolio Website

Role: portfolio owner; requirements, content, and review.

Problem

Professional background, achievements, interests, and career direction needed a clear and consistent home online.

Approach

Organized the information across linked pages, with consistent navigation and layouts adapted for smaller screens.

Outcome

Created a multi-page portfolio with a profile, experience, achievements, project showcase, and contact form that prepares an email draft.

Skills and lesson

Requirements planning, content organization, HTML, CSS, and responsive design review. Focused pages make a broad professional story easier to scan.

03

Data analysis · DHL

Claims Cost Reporting

Role: Data Analyst.

Problem

It was difficult and time-consuming to find total outgoing costs for damaged-item claims.

Approach

Used Power BI to make the total costs for damaged items easier and quicker to find.

Outcome

The claims process became more streamlined, without relying on unverified performance figures.

Skills and lesson

Power BI, data analysis, and cost reporting. Making key totals easy to locate can reduce friction in a recurring process.

The DHL case study is generalized and excludes confidential company data. Gauchini is a restaurant website concept; its menu, contact details, and social links are illustrative.
