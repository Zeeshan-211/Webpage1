Restaurant website concept

Gauchini

A dark, elegant restaurant experience with a home page, sample menu, story, and table booking page.

Explore the Gauchini project  ↗

Business context

A restaurant's first impression starts online.

For an independent restaurant, a useful website can help new guests discover the venue, understand its food and atmosphere, and plan a visit. The Gauchini project goal is to create a polished, mobile-friendly experience that brings those decisions together in one place.

The need

Turn interest into a visit.

Guests need a quick way to get a feel for the restaurant, browse its menu, learn about the concept, and find the next step toward a table.

The solution

One connected restaurant experience.

Responsive pages give each part of the guest journey a clear place:

  • Home
  • Menu
  • About
  • Bookings

Data-minded. Security-focused. Always learning.
