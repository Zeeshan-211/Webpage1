About Gauchini

An imagined Italian dining room with a modern point of view and a generous sense of occasion.

A restaurant concept

A little theatre. A lot of heart.

Gauchini is a restaurant website concept built around the pleasure of a long Italian dinner. It imagines a room with low, warm light, a table set with care, and food that invites conversation.

The cooking takes inspiration from familiar Italian flavours, with seasonal ingredients and handmade details at the centre. The mood is polished but never formal: an evening can be special without needing a reason.

Seasonal by nature

A menu imagined around ingredients at their best, with Italian tradition as its starting point.

Made to be shared

Generous plates, good conversation, and space at the table for one more.

Evenings with ease

Thoughtful hospitality and a relaxed pace, from the first course to the last.
